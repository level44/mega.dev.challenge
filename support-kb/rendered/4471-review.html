Different mechanisms, different fixes

Shared parser: day-first tenants supply day/month dates, but the shared parser reads month first. For example, harbour.dental’s app report R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July; lantern.row’s R-41203 describes 2 December becoming 12 February. Fix the shared parsing contract and verify the tenant’s intended date survives. See parser ticket.

Form hint: booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY, while its day-first form reads input in the tenant’s order. Following the hint makes the user enter month/day and the form interprets day/month. This affects form reports from alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour. R-41230, R-41241 and R-41251 are examples. Align the displayed hint with the actual form contract; a shared-parser fix alone does not establish this is repaired. See form-hint ticket.

These are mechanisms described by the exported executable model, not a reproduction against the production product. The individual one-day-like reports remain explicitly uncertain above.
